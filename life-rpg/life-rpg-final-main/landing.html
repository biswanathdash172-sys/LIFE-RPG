Why Life RPG Works

Traditional habit trackers feel like chores. Life RPG borrows what makes games addictive — visible progress, streak momentum, and small wins that compound — and applies it to your real daily routines. No cheating your own stats: every action is logged and verified.

Built for Consistency, Not Perfection

Missing a day doesn't erase your progress. Life RPG's streak system rewards showing up more often than it punishes an off day, so you build a sustainable routine instead of an all-or-nothing cycle that burns out in a week.
